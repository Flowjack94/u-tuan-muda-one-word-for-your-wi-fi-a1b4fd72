## The entry

**`diva`**

## Why it fits the brief

Home Wi-Fi behaves like a diva: it only works when and where *it* wants,
throws a tantrum at the worst possible moment (mid-call, mid-download), and
demands you stay close to the router to keep it happy. It is funny at once,
true to how home Wi-Fi actually behaves, and a fresh angle — the field so far
is crowded with ghosting, gaslighting, liar, and peekaboo, and none of those
capture the "only performs on its own terms" truth. It also works perfectly
as a network name: `diva` is the SSID your router deserves.

## How it works

`main.py` is a small deterministic program that writes exactly `diva` plus a
trailing newline to `/output/entry.txt`.
